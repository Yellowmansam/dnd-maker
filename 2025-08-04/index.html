D&D 5e Character Builder

Step-by-step flow inspired by D&D Beyond: Race → Class → Ability Scores → Background.

    1. Choose Race

    Select a race to review its languages, features, proficiencies, and racial bonuses.

    2. Choose Class

    Pick your primary class and review what it gains by level. Optionally add multiclass levels.

    3. Ability Scores (27-Point Buy)

    Use standard 5e point buy limits: scores from 8 to 15 before racial bonuses.

    Point Buy Rules

    • Total budget: 27 points
    • Minimum score: 8
    • Maximum score: 15 (before racial bonuses)

    4. Choose Background

    Select a background and review what it grants your character.
